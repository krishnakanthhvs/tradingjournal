Reconcile with your contract note

The standard estimate assumes one entry order and one exit order. For multiple orders, enter total brokerage (Other supports a flat per-order rate). Allocate delivery DP charges once per applicable debit, excluding GST; enter zero if allocated elsewhere. Broker discounts, minimums and daily rounding can change the final bill.

Use actual charges for ETFs, agricultural commodities, currency, BSE, physical settlement, exercised or assigned options, NRI accounts, MTF, dealer orders and other special cases. Account subscriptions, penalties and financing costs are not automatically included. MCX prices must use the correct contract multiplier in “units per lot”.

Your saved breakdown is retained with the trade. Gross P&L − total charges = net P&L; goals, win rates and calendars use net results.
